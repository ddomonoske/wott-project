\section{Notation, Units, and Parameters}
\label{sec:notation}

\Cref{tab:notation} lists the symbols used throughout this document, their
meaning, their SI unit, their default or typical value where one exists, and
the corresponding function, class, or entity attribute in the implementation.
Derivations
throughout this document use SI units exclusively, with one display-only
exception for reported velocity (\cref{sec:sim-outputs}).
